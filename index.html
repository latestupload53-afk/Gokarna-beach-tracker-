Before you head out

Small tips. Better trips.

Keep the coast enjoyable for you, other visitors and the people who call Gokarna home.

🌦️

Check conditions

Check weather, sea conditions and local advice. Avoid cliff edges and coastal trails during rough weather.

💧

Carry essentials

Bring drinking water, sun protection, suitable footwear and a charged phone for your outing.

🌿

Leave no trace

Take your rubbish with you, respect local communities and follow signs or restricted-access notices.
